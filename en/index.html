How to book a visit

  1. Call us or send the form

    22 863 21 21 or the online form. Tell us briefly what is wrong with the car.

  2. We confirm the date

    We call you back during working hours (Mon–Fri 8:00–17:00) and agree on a day.

  3. You bring the car in

    You are welcome to watch the repair yourself. For larger jobs the price is open to negotiation.
