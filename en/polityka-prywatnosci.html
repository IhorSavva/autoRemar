Privacy policy

Short and to the point: what data the form collects and why.

Data controller

The controller of your personal data is AUTO-REMAR, Bartosz Smakowski and Marek Smakowski, ul. Budki Szczęśliwickie 65, 02-460 Warszawa, Poland, e-mail: autoremar@autoremar.pl, phone 22 863 21 21.

What we collect

Through the “Book a visit” form we collect your name, phone number, e-mail address (optional), car make, model and year, the service you need, your preferred date and your comment.

Purpose and legal basis

We use the data only to contact you and arrange your visit (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR). We do not send advertising and we do not sell data.

Who processes the data for us

Requests are stored in Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.), and the notification about a new request is e-mailed through EmailJS. The map on this site comes from Google Maps and the fonts from Google Fonts; these services may record technical data such as your IP address.

How long

We keep the data for up to 12 months after your visit, or less if you withdraw consent.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and transfer your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to autoremar@autoremar.pl. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (Prezes UODO).
