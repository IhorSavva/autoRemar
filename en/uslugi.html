Complete timing kits, including 1.9 CDTI engines.

Brakes

Discs, pads, brake fluid. We check the whole system, not just the part that squeaks.

When changing pads we also inspect discs, lines and callipers. Brake fluid is replaced with a full bleed of the system.

Computer diagnostics
